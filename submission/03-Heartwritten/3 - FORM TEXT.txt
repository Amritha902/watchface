DESIGN NAME
Heartwritten

BRIEF DESIGN CONCEPT (theme mentioned)
For the Open Innovation theme, Heartwritten draws the time with your own heartbeat: a single glowing ECG line runs across the screen and rises into the digits, so the '1' is literally a heartbeat spike and every beat travels through the numbers. Resting, the line is calm and rose-red; in a workout it turns amber, beats harder and the card shows your heart-rate zone. Always-on keeps the same line as a thin grey trace on true black.
