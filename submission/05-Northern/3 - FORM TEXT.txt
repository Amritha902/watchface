DESIGN NAME
Northern

BRIEF DESIGN CONCEPT (theme mentioned)
For the Open Innovation theme, Northern brings the aurora to your wrist: green, teal and violet ribbons move slowly over snow-capped mountains and a still lake that reflects them. A hairline Inter Tight clock floats above the scene for a calm, premium read, and the complication suggests stepping outside or shows your documentary's progress. Motion is deliberately slow to stay restful, and always-on drops to thin grey type on true black.
