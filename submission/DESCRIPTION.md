# Encore — design package

**Participant:** Amritha S
**Design name:** Encore
**Tool used:** Figma (vector frames; this package mirrors the Figma file)
**Theme / Category:** Open Innovation (personalised, entertainment-driven smart face)
**Figma file:** https://www.figma.com/design/B5hYSaEPQLPnF5OnTFh9uD
**Resolution:** 368 × 448 rectangular OLED (Titan design-kit size)

## Concept (2–3 sentences)
Encore is a watch face that knows what you love: when you start a new series, your watch gifts you a face designed around that show's world, and you can pick a favourite character or describe a face in one sentence to have it made for you. The time is always the hero, set large and high-contrast for a one-second glance, while a single glass complication carries heart rate, steps or your episode progress. Every element is vector, every text is ≥21 px and every icon ≥25 px, and the always-on state drops to true black with type only.

## Concept (50–100 words, Adobe-track format)
Encore turns your watch into something that knows what you love. When you start a new series, your watch gifts you a face designed around that show's world, complete with live episode progress. Pick a favourite character and Encore paints their world; describe a face in one sentence and it is designed for you; edit it in plain words. Glanceability comes first: oversized, high-contrast time, one glass complication, text ≥21 px, icons ≥25 px. Heart rate pulses live, a breathing guide appears after spikes, and the always-on state is true black with type only.
