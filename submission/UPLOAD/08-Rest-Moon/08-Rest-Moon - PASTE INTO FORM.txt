Challenge: Open Innovation

Design Name:
Rest Moon

Brief Design Concept:
Open Innovation. Rest Moon uses the moon's phase to show how well rested you are, not the real lunar phase. A full moon means you've slept enough. As sleep debt builds over the week, the moon wanes into a thin crescent. The card says "Sleep · 82% rested" with last night's hours, and a row of seven small moons shows the last seven nights at a glance. When the moon gets thin, the card suggests one thing, like "bed by 10:30". Always-on redraws the moon as a thin grey outline under the time.
