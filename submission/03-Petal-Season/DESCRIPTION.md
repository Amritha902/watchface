## Brief design concept (paste into the form)
For the Open Innovation theme, Petal Season is a slow, romantic face for people who binge love stories: blossoms frame the date and petals drift past a large stacked serif clock against a dusk-pink sky. The stacked Libre Baskerville time keeps glanceability high while the warm gradient and soft motion keep it calm, and the complication carries your episode or a gentle nudge. Always-on reduces it to grey type on true black.
