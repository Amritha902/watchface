DESIGN NAME
Orbit 9

BRIEF DESIGN CONCEPT (theme mentioned)
For the Open Innovation theme, Orbit 9 gives you your own small planet: a ringed world glows behind monospaced digits while a moon completes one orbit every minute, quietly doubling as a seconds cue. Coral and indigo give strong contrast to the white JetBrains Mono time, and a single glass complication shows heart rate, steps or your current episode. Always-on keeps only the time, and the medallion variant frames the planet in a porthole.
