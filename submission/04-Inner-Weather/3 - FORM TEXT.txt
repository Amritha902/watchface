DESIGN NAME
Inner Weather

BRIEF DESIGN CONCEPT (theme mentioned)
For the Open Innovation theme, Inner Weather turns your stress level into the sky behind the time: when your heart-rate variability is healthy it is a clear, sunlit morning, and as stress rises clouds gather until a storm breaks. Instead of another number to decode, you feel your state at a glance, and a storm offers a one-tap breathing break. Always-on keeps the time and a single word forecast on true black.
