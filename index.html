Watch Face Design Challenge 2026 · Open Innovation

Encore.

A watch face that knows what you love. It notices what you're watching, gifts you a face made for it, and designs one from a sentence.

Drag the screen sideways to swipe · tap a card to wear it

1 Watch something

Encore's phone app sees what's playing on your streaming apps and builds a private taste profile on the phone. When a new favourite shows up, your watch gets a gift.

2 Or just describe it

Swipe to the last card on the watch, or type here. Encore turns a sentence into a full face: wallpaper, type and palette.

Your photo stays on your device and gets the selected show's styling: type, colour and the episode card.

3 Make it yours

Not quite right? Tell it what to change on the face you're wearing. Claude turns your words into exact edits, and repaints the artwork when you ask for something new.

4 It beats with you

Whatever face you wear, the heart rate pulses live. When your heart races, the screen edge glows red on every beat. After a sudden spike, a breathing guide takes over.

From binge to wrist.

No menus, no hunting through face galleries. Encore pays attention, then makes something just for you.

01 · NOTICE

Knows what's playing

On Android, the phone app reads what's playing on Netflix, Prime Video and other apps through the system's media controls. It only does this with your permission, and it all stays on the phone.

02 · LEARN

Builds your taste

The shows you finish, rewatch or binge build a private taste profile. Encore waits for a real favourite instead of reacting to every trailer.

03 · GIFT

A face, made for you

You glance down and see “Made for you · Because you're watching Friends.” One tap and you're wearing it, with your show's progress built in.

04 · CREATE

Or describe anything

“Rainy café”, “neon Tokyo”, “calm ocean”. Say it and Encore composes the wallpaper, typography and colour for you.

The collection.

Each face is a full scene, not a background image: the lights flicker, the vault turns, steam rises from the mug. They're all made of vectors, so they stay sharp and light on the battery.

All artwork here is original. It evokes each show's world without using logos, stills or likenesses. In production, official artwork would come through studio licensing, a natural partnership for Titan.

Built to ship.

Private by design

Viewing data never leaves the phone. The watch only receives finished faces. Every connection is opt-in and can be revoked.

Honest about platforms

Android's media controls and notification access make automatic detection possible. iOS doesn't allow it, so iPhone users connect by picking shows or importing their viewing history.

Always-on aware

The always-on view drops the wallpaper to true black and keeps only thin type, which saves battery and avoids burn-in.

Generator, not a gallery

Faces are built from parts (motif, palette, type, layout) instead of image files. A face can be made in seconds on the phone, and new shows only need new motifs.
