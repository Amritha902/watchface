The idea

Every smartwatch gives us the time to the second. Hardly any tell us what kind of time it is. Indian tradition split the day into eight prahars of about three hours: four for daylight, four for night, each measured from the actual sunrise. PRAHAR brings that idea onto the wrist. Your day becomes eight glanceable chapters tied to the real sky, and each chapter carries a quiet nudge drawn from modern circadian science.

Beyond the dial

There are no hands and no 12-hour circle. The sun (or the moon, in its real phase) travels a 24-hour ring: noon at the top, midnight at the bottom, sunrise on the left, sunset on the right.

A dial that breathes

The prahars are computed from your real sunrise and sunset. In Oslo in June, the daylight chapters stretch to almost 5 hours. In December they shrink to 90 minutes. No two days look exactly alike.

Heritage × wellness

Each prahar pairs a traditional name with a nudge: Brahma muhurta for breathing, Madhyahna for deep focus, Pradosha for unplugging. It's a wellness coach that never sends a notification.

Anatomy of the face

Built for a 466 × 466 round AMOLED panel. All the information sits inside three rings, and you can read it in under a second.

1Prahar ring

Eight coloured arcs, one per prahar. The current one fills up as it passes, so you can see how much of this chapter is left.

2Sun / Moon hand

The single "hand" is the sun by day. By night it becomes the moon, drawn in tonight's real phase.

3Sky background

The gradient follows the real sky: indigo pre-dawn, rose dawn, blue noon, golden hour, then stars come out at night.

4Sunrise, sunset & Brahma muhurta

Gold dots mark today's sunrise and sunset. A pink arc marks Brahma muhurta, the 48-minute window that ends 48 minutes before sunrise.

5Time + prahar + nudge

Large digital time, the prahar name in English and Devanagari, a contextual nudge chip, and a countdown to the next prahar.

6Health complications

Heart rate, steps and battery, kept quiet at the bottom. The always-on mode drops to thin outlines on true black to protect battery and avoid burn-in.

Why it can ship

Offline & light

Sunrise and sunset use the NOAA solar approximation: a few lines of math, no network and no GPS polling. Location is taken once from the paired phone.

Platform-ready

The face is pure vector (arcs, text, gradients) and maps directly onto Watch Face Format / Watch Face Studio and onto Titan's own smart-watch face pipelines.

Bilingual by design

English and हिंदी ship from day one, and the prahar names can be localised to Tamil, Bengali, Marathi and more for Titan's pan-India audience.
